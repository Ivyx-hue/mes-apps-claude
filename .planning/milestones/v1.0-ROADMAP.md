**Goal:** The owner can survol the entire Bachelor QHSE at CESI Bordeaux in one scroll — 1-minute pitch, programme par année, RNCP blocs cited by fiche number + version date, alternance calendar, métiers with sourced salary ranges — without ever seeing an unsourced fact or a generic-CESI sentence dressed up as Bordeaux-specific.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on:** Phase 1 (chassis + identity must be locked before long-form prose lands)
**Requirements:** DECOUV-01, DECOUV-02, DECOUV-03, DECOUV-04, DECOUV-05, DECOUV-06, DECOUV-07, DECOUV-08
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A first-time visitor reads the Accueil section in under 30 seconds and can answer "what is this site, who is it for, what's inside" without scrolling further.
  2. The owner clicks into Découverte and sees the formation summarized in ~1 minute of reading (durée, niveau, RNCP, rythme alternance) before any deeper module breakdown.
  3. The owner clicks any entry in the in-section mini-TOC and lands on the right subsection (programme, RNCP blocs, calendrier, métiers).

## Phase Ordering Rationale

- **Skeleton before content** because sticky-positioning bugs, smooth-scroll anchor offsets, and burger-menu-stays-open are bug-prone and faster to diagnose on an empty page than under 25 cards plus a 1500-word pitch.
- **Découverte before Biblio** because Découverte is hand-written prose (no render pipeline, validates typography on real long-form text); Biblio is a data + render system (different mental model, finishes faster once the prose section is locked).
- **5 × 5 Biblio cards before any v1.1 enhancements** because PROJECT.md `Out of Scope` is explicit: interactive study tools deferred to V2 once V1 reading hub is shipped and validated in real study sessions. Scrollspy, filter chips, mark-as-read, Ctrl+K search all live in `V2_BACKLOG.md`, not in V1.

## Out-of-Roadmap Notes

- Each phase ends with a `git push origin main` to the existing GitHub Actions pipeline; live URL ~60 s later.
- The existing QHSE Trainer at the repo root is treated as a frozen sibling — no modifications, only co-existence.
- No backend, no Supabase, no auth in V1 — static HTML is sufficient (despite Supabase env vars existing in the repo for other apps).
